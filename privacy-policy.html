Doc — Privacy Policy Effective 14 Sep 2026

Content Auto-Poster

A personal, single-user application built and operated by Jamie Boylan, used solely to publish their own videos to their own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not distributed to, or used by, anyone else.

01

Scope

This policy covers the single application described above. It is the only product this policy applies to, and it has no other users besides its owner.

02

What data this application accesses

When connected to the owner's accounts, the application requests only the permissions needed to upload and publish video content the owner submits through it:

  • YouTube — upload access via the YouTube Data API (scope youtube.upload), used to publish videos to the connected channel.
  • TikTok — video publishing access via the Content Posting API (scopes video.publish, video.upload), used to upload videos to the connected account.

No other data is read, collected, or processed from these accounts — no analytics, followers, messages, or other users' content.

03

How data is stored

Authorization tokens issued by YouTube and TikTok are stored locally on the owner's own computer only. They are never transmitted to or stored on any third-party server — this application has no backend server of its own.

04

Data sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party. Video files and metadata (title, description, tags, captions) are sent only to YouTube and TikTok, as directed by the owner, to publish content the owner created.

05

Advertising and tracking

This application contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

06

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to jamieboylan87@gmail.com.
