Content Auto-Poster

A personal, single-user tool built and operated by Jamie Boylan.

Content Auto-Poster runs locally on its owner's own computer. It lets the owner attach a finished video along with a title, description and caption, then publishes it to their own YouTube channel and their own TikTok account in one step. It has no public users, no sign-up, and no server component beyond the owner's local machine — it exists purely to save its owner the repetitive work of uploading the same video to two platforms by hand.
